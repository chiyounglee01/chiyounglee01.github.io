(It may take a minute for the app to load.) <br></br><h1><b>Overview</b></h1><br></br><p><b>Goal:</b> Build a monthly monitoring app to measure laptop brand visibility and ranking in LLM generated recommendations. This app can be used to measure if a brand's generative engine optimization strategies are having an immediate effect on AI recommendations.</p><p><b>Process:</b> The app submits 500 questions on laptop recommendations to the following LLM models (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) every month. It captures the top three recommended brands for every answer. It visualizes the sum of each brand's 1st recommendation over time. It also calculates a weighted visibility score (0.5 for 1st, 0.3 for 2nd, and 0.2 for 3rd) over time. </p><p><b>Tools:</b> Python, Pandas, LLM APIs (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini), n8n, Docker, SQL, Supabase (for data hosting), Streamlit (for app UI).</p>
